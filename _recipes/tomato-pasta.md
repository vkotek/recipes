A quick tomato sauce for a weeknight dinner. Serves 2.

### Ingredients

- 200 g pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes
- Salt and black pepper
- Grated parmesan or fresh basil, to serve

### Method

1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the package directions.
2. Warm the olive oil in a pan and cook the garlic for about 30 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Drain the pasta, toss it with the sauce, and serve with parmesan or basil.
