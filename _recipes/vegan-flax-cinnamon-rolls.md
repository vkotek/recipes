A pillowy, bakery-style vegan cinnamon roll built on a high-hydration flax dough — soft, glossy, and made for a cold morning with a hot drink. Yields 6 generously sized rolls.

**Make-ahead tip:** pause after arranging the rolls in the baking dish, cover, and refrigerate overnight. Bring them out, let them do their second rise, then bake.

**Gluten-free option:** use a 1:1 all-purpose GF flour blend (with xanthan gum) and add 60–120 ml extra warm plant milk, as GF blends absorb more moisture. Gram measurements are more accurate than cups if you have a scale.

[Original recipe source](https://halleburns.substack.com/p/my-vegan-flax-cinnamon-roll-recipe).

### Equipment

- Stand mixer with dough hook (or mix by hand)
- Large mixing bowl
- Rolling pin
- Parchment-lined baking dish
- Kitchen scale (recommended)

### Ingredients

- Bread flour — 360 g (3 cups)
- Ground flaxseed — 1 tbsp (7 g)
- Water (for flax egg) — 3 tbsp (45 g)
- Warm plant-based milk — 180 g (¾ cup)
- Soft vegan butter — 56 g (½ stick)
- Cane sugar — 50 g (¼ cup)
- Instant yeast — 2 tsp (or one 7 g packet)
- Vanilla extract — 1 tsp
- Salt — ½ tsp
- Soft vegan butter (filling) — 56 g (½ stick)
- Light brown sugar (filling) — 100 g (½ cup)
- Ground cinnamon (filling) — 1 tbsp
- Powdered sugar (icing) — 180 g (1½ cups)
- Soft vegan butter (icing) — 42 g (2–3 tbsp)
- Plant-based milk (icing) — 30 g (2 tbsp)

### Method

1. Make the flax egg: stir the ground flaxseed with the water and let sit for 5–10 minutes until thickened.
2. Combine all dough ingredients in a bowl and mix with a stand mixer and dough hook for 6–8 minutes, or by hand for about 10 minutes, until a sticky, glossy dough ball forms.
3. Test the gluten: a small piece of dough should stretch thin and translucent without tearing.
4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise until tripled in size — about 2–3 hours.
5. Mix the filling ingredients together until smooth and spreadable.
6. Punch down the dough, roll it out to about 1 cm thick, and spread the filling evenly across the surface.
7. Slice into 2–3 inch strips, roll each one up, and place them in a parchment-lined baking dish with the rolls touching each other so they rise upward.
8. Cover and let rise for 1–2 hours, then bake at 175 °C (350 °F) for 20–25 minutes until golden.
9. Beat the icing ingredients until smooth and spread over the rolls while they are still warm from the oven.

### Storage

Best eaten the day they're baked. Store leftovers in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or freeze the baked (un-iced) rolls for up to 2 months. Reheat at 150 °C for 8–10 minutes and ice after warming.

### Vegan nutritionist comment

These are a classic indulgent bake — high in carbs and fat from the butter and sugar, with modest protein (~9 g per roll) coming from the bread flour and the flax egg. The ground flaxseed adds a small omega-3 and fiber boost, but not enough to offset the sugar load. For a more balanced breakfast, enjoy one roll alongside a protein-rich soy latte or a side of tofu scramble, and save the rest for later.
