### Ingredients

- Edamame (shelled, cooked and cooled) — 250 g
- Ripe avocado — 2 medium
- Garlic — 1 clove, minced
- Fresh lime juice — 1 tbsp
- Fresh cilantro (or basil) — 15 g, chopped
- Olive oil — 1 tbsp
- Sea salt and black pepper — to taste
- Red pepper flakes or sesame seeds — for topping (optional)

### Method

1. Place the shelled, cooked edamame in a food processor and pulse until coarsely ground.
2. Add the avocado flesh, garlic, lime juice, cilantro, and olive oil to the food processor.
3. Pulse until the mixture reaches your desired consistency (smooth or slightly chunky).
4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5. Serve immediately or store in the fridge for up to 2 days.

### Vegan nutritionist comment

This spread is excellent for boosting your intake of plant-based protein and healthy monounsaturated fats. The edamame provides a great protein and fiber base, while the avocado adds creaminess and essential nutrients. Pair it with whole-grain bread or raw vegetable sticks for a complete, satisfying snack.
