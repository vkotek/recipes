A quick, no-cook spread that comes together in minutes and packs a serious garlicky punch plus a good dose of protein from the tofu.

### Equipment

- Immersion blender (or food processor)
- Mixing bowl

### Ingredients

- Firm tofu — 200 g (1 block), drained
- Vegan yogurt — 60 ml (4 tbsp)
- Garlic — 4 cloves, minced
- Olive oil — 15 ml (1 tbsp)
- Nutritional yeast — 15 g (2 big tbsp)
- Himalayan salt — to taste
- Fresh chives (or similar greens) — 15 g (small handful), chopped

### Method

1. Drain and pat the tofu dry, then crumble it into a bowl.
2. Add the vegan yogurt, minced garlic, olive oil, nutritional yeast, and a pinch of Himalayan salt.
3. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth and creamy. Taste and adjust salt.
4. Fold in the chopped chives.
5. Chill for 10–15 minutes to let the flavours meld, then serve on bread, crackers, or as a dip.

### Storage

Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Stir before serving.

### Vegan nutritionist comment

Tofu is the star here, delivering complete plant protein plus calcium and iron. The nutritional yeast adds B vitamins (look for a B12-fortified brand) and a savoury, cheesy depth. Pair it with a source of vitamin C — fresh tomato or bell pepper on your bread — to boost iron absorption. For extra omega-3s, sprinkle with a little hemp or flaxseed before serving.
