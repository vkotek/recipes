---
title: Simple pancakes
prep_time: 5 minutes
cook_time: 15 minutes
---

Fluffy everyday pancakes. Makes about 8.

### Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- A pinch of salt
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

### Method

1. Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. In another bowl, whisk the egg, milk, and melted butter.
3. Stir the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients until just combined.
4. Cook small scoops in a lightly buttered pan over medium heat, flipping once bubbles form.
